## 1. Summary

**The admin P1 scope is half verified and not complete.** Of 567 P1 requirements, 288 pass with tests or a recorded live run, and code exists for 81 %. The money and operations core is solid: staff roles and limits, credit adjustments, refunds, pricing publish with Stripe sync and rollback, view-as, tickets with macros, jobs, webhook replay, reconciliation, kill switches and the audit log. P2 is 9 % verified and mostly not started, as planned.

**Three requirements fail outright:**

1. **Any staff role can export the audit log.** `GET /admin/audit/export` has no `@StaffRoles` and never checks `can_export`, although §2.2 limits exports to Super Admin, Ops and Finance. The end-to-end test exports as the read-only role and expects success (`backend/api/src/modules/admin/admin.controllers.ts:960`; `d12-admin.e2e.test.ts:1934`). Verified directly.
2. **Support can pause agents and numbers.** §2.2 allows Support to pause a tenant only, with a ticket. QA-034 widened it to agents and numbers; that is recorded in CLAUDE.md but in no ADR.
3. **Customer data appears in console URLs.** Seven console pages write the search text, such as phone digits and customer names, into the query string (e.g. `frontend/admin/app/calls/page.tsx:38-39`), against §10 "no customer data in URLs".

**The P1 gaps that matter most:**

- **§2.1 and §10 hardening:** no re-auth before pricing publish, refunds over $500 or role changes; no IP allowlist; no WAF or CSP on the console distribution; no watermark on exports.
- **Role limits stored but never enforced:** `can_change_plan`, `can_export`, `can_delete_customer_data` and the agent-stack limits change nothing on the server. Support's credit cap applies per action, not per ticket, and Support can adjust credits with no ticket.
- **Staff text on credit adjustments never reaches the customer.** `customer_text` is stored, but D5 shows only a generic line, against §1 principle 4.
- **Customer 360 is incomplete:** no Notes, Leads & dialing or Danger tabs; no anonymise-and-delete; no plan-tier change, flags, resend invite, ownership transfer, lot-expiry extension or waive rental; the Agents tab has only pause.
- **SA8 recordings:** no staff "Listen" with a reason, no delete, and the call detail lacks transcript, analysis, cost breakdown, prompt snapshot and webhook timeline.
- **SA11 P1 parts:** the global phone blocklist is enforced on calls but has no staff screen or API; KYC is a per-customer card, not a queue, with no "request info" or re-verification.
- **SA4:** no Unassigned or SLA-breach views; statuses and categories differ from §7.2 with no ADR; no system-generated tickets (dispute, failed activation, three failed top-ups); staff replies are not emailed; no attachments; no "resend emails" macro; thin context panel.
- **SA5:** the margin floor is checked per tier against the dearest pack, so a cheaper pack under the floor can still publish; no default 35 % floor until cost inputs are entered; cost inputs are two blended rates, not the itemised table; no version compare; no Slack diff on publish; no warning when a cap falls below live usage.
- **SA1, SA7, SA12, SA13, SA14 detail:** no gross margin, churn, calls-per-hour chart or Today/MTD ranges; no voice-platform account panel, hourly peak chart, orphaned-KB clean-up or skip-step; no queue pause or environment info; support hours, signature and SLA targets are constants; the global audit log shows staff actions only.

**Correction to the portal audit:** the SA3 user actions (verify email, lock, unlock, reset MFA, force logout) were run against the real customer dev tenant on 28 Sep (ADR 0021 §7). Row AD-18 in the portal audit is now PASS.
